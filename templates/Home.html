How it works

From first call to switched on

A fixed sequence, so you always know what happens next and who's doing it.

01 — Week 1

Site assessment

An engineer reviews your roof, your municipal bill and your load profile to size the system correctly.

02 — Week 1

System design

You get a proposal with panel layout, battery capacity, output estimates and a fixed price, in writing.

03 — Week 2–3

Installation

Certified electricians complete the install and handle Certificate of Compliance and municipal sign-off.

04 — Ongoing

Monitoring & support

You track generation from an app, and our support line covers callouts for the life of the warranty.
